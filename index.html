About the artist

Behind the Art

ARTIST CODE · A1740001

Roberto R. Agcaoili Jr. is a Filipino visual artist and a Fine Arts graduate, major in Advertising, from the University of Santo Tomas (UST) in Manila, Philippines.

His creative interests include imagination, symbolism, and the exploration of visual ideas. His Military Robots collection, also known as Robot Sheep, presents robotic forms that invite viewers to look beyond their appearance.

A1740001 Gallery RAJ is an evolving space for his original works and future collections. A fuller artist statement will be added as the collection develops.
